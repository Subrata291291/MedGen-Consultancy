Privacy Policy

How RevOn Business Solutions collects, protects, uses, and safeguards your personal and organizational information.

1. Information We Collect

RevOn Business Solutions collects information to provide higher quality healthcare technology and revenue cycle services to our clients. We may collect:

  • Contact Information: Name, work email address, telephone number, job title, and organization name when submitting contact or consultation forms.
  • Technical & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and site navigation metrics collected via web analytics.
  • Service-Related Data: Operational documentation provided by healthcare payers or providers for consultation and workflow assessment.

2. How We Use Collected Information

We utilize collected data strictly for professional business purposes:

  • To deliver, configure, and manage healthcare services, claims testing, and RCM solutions.
  • To respond to consultation requests, billing inquiries, and customer support communications.
  • To analyze website performance and enhance user experience.
  • To satisfy legal, statutory, and regulatory compliance obligations.

3. Healthcare Data & HIPAA Alignment

RevOn Business Solutions operates in strict compliance with HIPAA privacy and security rules. Protected Health Information (PHI) processed on behalf of covered entities is governed exclusively by executed Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) and is subject to rigorous physical, administrative, and technical safeguards.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

RevOn Business Solutions does not sell, rent, or trade personal or client information to third parties. We share information only with trusted service providers under strict confidentiality agreements, or when required by law or judicial order.

5. Data Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS), firewalls, access management controls, and secure data storage to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

Privacy & Data Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data privacy rights, please contact our Privacy Team at contact@revonbusinesssolutions.com.
