Compliance Policy

RevOn Business Solutions is committed to upholding strict regulatory compliance, data privacy, and operational integrity across all US Healthcare payer and provider engagements.

1. Regulatory Alignment & Standards

RevOn Business Solutions operates in full alignment with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health (HITECH) Act, and applicable US federal and state healthcare regulations. We mandate strict administrative, physical, and technical safeguards across all operational workflows.

2. Business Associate Agreements (BAA)

Before embarking on any operational, payer configuration, testing, or revenue cycle engagement involving Protected Health Information (PHI), RevOn executes a formal Business Associate Agreement (BAA) detailing explicit data handling responsibilities, permitted disclosures, and reporting protocols.

3. Data Security & Access Governance

We enforce role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA), and secure encrypted channels for all data transmission and storage. Operational access to client EHR, PM, and payer adjudication platforms is strictly limited to authorized personnel assigned to designated accounts.

4. Workforce Training & Confidentiality

All RevOn specialists undergo mandatory healthcare compliance training upon onboarding and periodic annual refresher certifications. Employees sign binding non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) and adhere to strict clean-desk policies and workstation security protocols.

5. Auditing & Incident Management

Our compliance officer leads regular operational audits to evaluate data handling procedures and queue integrity. In the event of any suspected security incident or compliance discrepancy, RevOn follows an immediate response protocol to investigate, contain, and report findings to affected clients in accordance with statutory requirements.

Compliance Office Inquiries

If you have specific compliance questions or wish to request our BAA documentation, please contact our team at contact@revonbusinesssolutions.com.
